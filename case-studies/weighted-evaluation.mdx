## Recommendation

Mintlify is recommended for Rejesha Green because it provides the strongest balance of GitHub-based documentation, fast deployment, search, modern presentation, and AI-oriented capabilities while meeting the assignment's free-feature constraint.

GitBook is a strong alternative for teams that prioritize a hosted editing experience. Docusaurus is appropriate when maximum control and self-hosting are more important than setup simplicity.
