This guide explains how developers should understand, modify, test, document, and deploy changes to the Rejesha Green platform.

## Before starting work

Before making a change:

1. Understand the business requirement.
2. Identify the affected users and business process.
3. Identify the affected frontend, backend, database, or integration component.
4. Review the relevant technical documentation.
5. Check whether the change affects authentication, permissions, or personal data.
6. Confirm the acceptance criteria.
7. Identify how the change will be tested.
